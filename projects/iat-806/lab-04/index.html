Lab 04 — The Dance Floor

An interactive dance floor featuring two different dances, colorful dancers, and animations that react to the mouse.

My Sketch

How to Play

Move your mouse around the dance floor to change the dancers' speeds. Dancers closer to the mouse move faster, while those farther away move more slowly. The grid alternates between my original dance and a jumping animation.
